BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A little clarity.

Where will the competitions take place?

Competitions are held within the Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) campus.

How many places are available at VIT?

VIT has 25 Essay places, 15 PPT team places and 25 Poster places. Each PPT topic accepts up to three teams. Remaining places are displayed above.

Can I change my college on a registration form?

The college is fixed to the link you opened and is validated on the server. Use your own institution’s dedicated link.

What happens at the deadline?

This portal stops accepting new registrations at 5:00 PM IST on 16 November 2026, including competitions and topics with remaining places.

How do I know my registration succeeded?

A success message and unique registration ID appear after the database records your entry. Save or print this confirmation. Submitting the form alone does not confirm a place.
